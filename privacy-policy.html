Privacy Policy

Application: Network Speed Monitor (com.netspeed.monitor)
Last Updated: August 11, 2026
Core Privacy Guarantee: Network Speed Monitor is an entirely offline, privacy-first utility. We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, browsing activity, or network content.

1. Information Collection & Usage

Network Speed Calculation: The app measures live upload and download throughput using Android’s standard public API (android.net.TrafficStats). These counters measure the total number of bytes transferred at the Linux kernel socket level across your Wi-Fi and Cellular connections.

On-Device Only: All calculations, moving average smoothing, and session counters are computed locally in your device's memory. The app never inspects the content of your internet traffic, URLs, or files, and never sends data to any server.

2. Permissions Used and Why

Permission Purpose
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Displays live download and upload speeds in the phone's status bar and notification shade.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC Maintains continuous, reliable background throughput calculation on modern Android versions (Android 14 & 15).
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Detects whether the device is connected to Wi-Fi or Mobile Data.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Restores the speed monitor on device boot (only if enabled by the user).
REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS Allows the user to exempt the monitor from Samsung One UI / Android background sleep limits.

3. Third-Party Services & Tracking

  • No Advertisements: We do not integrate ad networks.
  • No Analytics SDKs: We do not use third-party analytics (e.g., Firebase, Google Analytics, Facebook SDK).
  • Zero Tracking: The app does not track your location, device ID, or user behavior.

4. Data Retention

Your user preferences (such as unit choice and display preferences) are stored locally in Android's private SharedPreferences. Uninstalling the app completely wipes all data from your device.

5. Children’s Privacy

The application does not collect any personally identifiable information and is safe for users of all ages.
